\section{\bilingual{Proof of the Main Theorems}{主要定理的证明}}
\label{section:main-results}

\bilingual{
    This section is devoted to the proof of the asymptotic speed order of the minimum position of the $N$-branching random walk with infinite offspring. The assumptions in this section include \Cref{assumption:psi-zero,assumption:at-least-one-child,assumption:psi-one,assumption:psi-prime-one,assumption:finite-variance,assumption:log-expt-cross-sum-exp-neg-sum,assumption:moment-1,assumption:moment-4,assumption:moment-exp-1},~ i.e., $\psi(0)>0$ (supercritical regime), $\#\Xi\ge 1$ a.s. (at least one child), $\psi(1)=0$ (normalization), $\psi'(1)=0$ (zero mean of the spine process), $\mathbb E[\xi^2]=\sigma^2<\infty$ (finite variance), $\mathbb E[\sum_{i,j\in\mathbb N, i\neq j} e^{-(\Xi_i+\Xi_j)}]<\infty$ (finite cross Laplacian), $\mathbb E[(\Xi_1)_+]<\infty$ (finite positive part of the leftmost step), $\mathbb E[(\Xi_1)_+^4]<\infty$ (finite fourth moment of the positive part of the leftmost step), and there exists some $\lambda>0$ such that $\mathbb E[e^{\lambda \Xi_1}]<\infty$ (finite exponential moment of the leftmost step).
}{
    本节将给出无限分支 $N$-分支随机游走最左侧粒子位置渐进速率阶数的证明. 本节的假设包括\Cref{assumption:psi-zero,assumption:at-least-one-child,assumption:psi-one,assumption:psi-prime-one,assumption:finite-variance,assumption:log-expt-cross-sum-exp-neg-sum,assumption:moment-1,assumption:moment-4,assumption:moment-exp-1},~ 参见第 \ref{section:explanations-on-assumptions} 节的解释说明.
}

\bilingual{
    First, by using the several lemmas proved in the previous section, we show a stronger $\mathrm L^2$ convergence result as a tool for proving the main theorem. This generalizes Proposition 3.1 in A\"id\'ekon and Hu \mycite{aïdékon2025averageweightpercolationcompletegraph} to apply to more general assumptions.
}{
    首先,~ 利用上文证得的多个引理,~ 证明一个更强的$\mathrm L^2$收敛的工具性结论.~ 这推广了 A\"id\'ekon 与 Hu \mycite{aïdékon2025averageweightpercolationcompletegraph} 中的命题 3.1,~ 以适用于更一般的假设条件.
}


\noindent {\bf\Cref{proposition:3.1}.} 
\bilingual{
    Fix $a\in(0,1]$ and $t>0$. If $\mathcal V^N=\mathcal V^{N|a}$ starts with $\lfloor N^a\rfloor$ particles located at $0$, and its branching law $\Xi$ satisfies \Cref{assumption:psi-zero,assumption:at-least-one-child,assumption:psi-one,assumption:psi-prime-one,assumption:finite-variance,assumption:log-expt-cross-sum-exp-neg-sum,assumption:moment-1,assumption:moment-4}, then for any sequence $\ell_N$ satisfying $\lim_{N\to\infty}\frac{\ell_N}{\log^3N}=t$, 
}{
    固定 $a\in(0,1]$ 和 $t>0$. ~若 $N$-分支随机游走 $\mathcal V^N=\mathcal V^{N|a}$ 从 $\lfloor N^a\rfloor$ 个位于 $0$ 的粒子开始,~ 且其分支律$\Xi$满足\Cref{assumption:psi-zero,assumption:at-least-one-child,assumption:psi-one,assumption:psi-prime-one,assumption:finite-variance,assumption:log-expt-cross-sum-exp-neg-sum,assumption:moment-1,assumption:moment-4},~ 那么对于任何满足 $\displaystyle\lim_{N\to\infty}\textstyle\frac{\ell_N}{\log^3N}=t$ 的数列 $\ell_N$,
}
{
    \begin{equation*}
        \frac 1{\log N}
        \min_{|u|=\ell_N}
        \mathcal V^N(u)
        \xrightarrow{\mathrm L^2}
        -a+\frac{\pi^2}2\sigma^2t.
    \end{equation*}
}
\bilingual{
    Moreover, under an additional \Cref{assumption:moment-exp-1}, i.e., the existence of $\lambda>0$ such that $\mathbb E[e^{\lambda \Xi_1}]<\infty$, the $\mathrm L^2$ convergence holds for $a=0$.
}{
    另外,~ 在额外的\Cref{assumption:moment-exp-1} 下,~ 即存在 $\lambda>0$ 使得 $\mathbb E[e^{\lambda \Xi_1}]<\infty$,~ 该$\mathrm L^2$ 收敛对于 $a=0$ 也成立.
}

\bilingual{
    The meaning of this theorem is that, if we take the time axis to be of order $\log^3 N$ (i.e., time grows with $N$ at a log-cubed rate) and the one-dimensional space axis to be of order $\log N$, then, in this non-symmetrically logarithmically scaled time-space, there exists a line $-a+\frac{\pi^2}2\sigma^2t$, such that the minimum position of the $N$-branching random walk, at each vertical time slice $t$, converges in $\mathrm L^2$ sense to the point on the aforementioned line at that slice as $N$ increases.
}{
    这个定理的含义是说,~ 把时间轴取为 $t\log^3 N$ 的数量级 (即时间随着 $N$ 增加而以三次对数的速率增长),~ 把一维空间轴取为 $\log N$ 的数量级,~ 那么,~ 在这个经不对称对数缩放的时空中,~ 存在一条直线$-a+\frac{\pi^2}2\sigma^2t$,~ 使得$N$-分支随机游走的最左侧位置,~ 在每一个纵向的时间切片 $t$ 处,~ 随着$N$增加而在$\mathrm L^2$ 的意义下收敛到上述直线的该时间切片处的点.
}

\begin{proof}[\bilingual{Proof of \Cref{proposition:3.1}}{\Cref{proposition:3.1} 的证明}]
    \quad

    \bilingual{
        The result would follow if we can show that
    }{
        命题将得证,~ 若能证得
    }
    \begin{align}
        \lim_{N\to\infty}
        \frac 1{\log N}
        \mathbb E\left[
            \min_{|u|=\ell_N}\mathcal V^N(u)
        \right]
        &
        =
        -a+\frac{\pi^2}2\sigma^2t,
        \label{eq:4.13}
        \\
        \lim_{N\to\infty}
        \frac 1{\log^2 N}
        \mathrm{Var}\!\left[
            \min_{|u|=\ell_N}\mathcal V^N(u)
        \right]
        &
        =
        0.
        \label{eq:4.14}
    \end{align}
    \bilingual{
        Indeed, for
    }{
        事实上,~ 对
    }
    \begin{equation*}
        \mathcal M^N_n:=\min_{|u|=n}\mathcal V^N(u), \quad
        \mathcal X_N:=\frac 1{\log N}\mathcal M^N_{\ell_N}, \quad
        \mu=\mu(t):=-a+\frac{\pi^2}2\sigma^2t,
    \end{equation*}
    \bilingual{
        we have
    }{
        有
    }
    \begin{equation*}
        \mathbb E\left[
            \left(
                \mathcal X_N-\mu
            \right)^2
        \right]
        =
        \mathrm{Var}\!\left[
            \mathcal X_N
        \right]
        +
        \left(
            \mathbb E\left[
                \mathcal X_N
            \right]
            -\mu
        \right)^2
        \xrightarrow{}
        0,
        \quad
        N\to\infty.
    \end{equation*}

    \quad

    \bilingual{
        Further, it suffices to show that for any $\zeta<\mu<\zeta'$, there exists some $\varepsilon=\varepsilon(\zeta,\zeta')>0$ such that for some $N_0=N_0(\varepsilon,\zeta,\zeta')>0$ and all $N\ge N_0$,
    }{
        进一步,~ 只需证明对于任意 $\zeta<\mu<\zeta'$,~ 存在某个 $\varepsilon=\varepsilon(\zeta,\zeta')>0$ 使得对于某个 $N_0=N_0(\varepsilon,\zeta,\zeta')>0$ 和所有 $N\ge N_0$,
    }
    \begin{align}
        \mathbb P(\mathcal X_N\ge\zeta')
        =
        \mathbb P\left(
            \mathcal M^N_{\ell_N}
            \ge
            \zeta'\log N
        \right)
        &
        \le
        N^{-\varepsilon},
        \label{eq:4.15}
        \\
        \mathbb P(\mathcal X_N\le\zeta)
        =
        \mathbb P\left(
            \mathcal M^N_{\ell_N}
            \le
            \zeta\log N
        \right)
        &
        \le
        N^{-\varepsilon}.
        \label{eq:4.16}
    \end{align}
    \bilingual{
        Indeed, observe that $\mathcal M^N_n$ is (stochastically) smaller than $\mathcal V^1$ at time $n$, where $\mathcal V^1$ is the branching random walk in which only the leftmost particle is kept at each step. Therefore, $\mathcal V^1$ is a random walk with step distribution $\Xi_1$. Under \Cref{assumption:psi-one,assumption:moment-4} (i.e. $\psi(1)=0$ and $\mathbb E[(\Xi_1)_+^4]<\infty$), we can show that
    }{
        事实上,~ 注意到 $\mathcal M^N_n$ 小于 (或随机受制于) $\mathcal V^1$ 在时刻 $n$ 的位置,~ 其中 $\mathcal V^1$ 是每一步只保留最左侧粒子的分支随机游走.~ 因此,~ $\mathcal V^1$ 是一个步长分布为 $\Xi_1$ 的随机游走.~ 在\Cref{assumption:psi-one,assumption:moment-4} (即 $\psi(1)=0$ 和 $\mathbb E[(\Xi_1)_+^4]<\infty$) 下,~ 我们可以证明
    }
    \begin{equation*}
        \mathbb E[(\mathcal M^N_{n})_-^4]=O_n(1),
        \quad
        \mathbb E[(\mathcal M^N_{n})_+^4]=O_n(n^4),
    \end{equation*}
    \bilingual{
        as $n$ goes to infinity (see \Cref{lemma:M:moment-4:neg,lemma:M:moment-4:pos} for details), and thus
    }{
        在 $n$ 趋近于无穷大时成立 (详见\Cref{lemma:M:moment-4:neg,lemma:M:moment-4:pos}),~ 从而
    }
    \begin{equation*}
        \mathbb E[(\mathcal M^N_{n})^4]
        \le
        \mathbb E[(\mathcal M^N_{n})_-^4]
        +
        \mathbb E[(\mathcal M^N_{n})_+^4]
        =
        O_n(n^4).
    \end{equation*}
    \bilingual{
        Now, let for $N\ge 1$,
    }{
        现在,~ 对 $N\ge 1$,~ 令
    }
    \begin{equation*}
        H_N
        :=\left\{
            \zeta\log N\le \mathcal M^N_{\ell_N}\le \zeta'\log N
        \right\}
        =
        \left\{
            \zeta\le \mathcal X_N\le \zeta'
        \right\}.
    \end{equation*}
    \bilingual{
        According to \eqref{eq:4.15} and \eqref{eq:4.16}, for all $N\ge N_0$,
    }{
        根据\eqref{eq:4.15} 和 \eqref{eq:4.16},~ 对所有 $N\ge N_0$,
    }
    $
        \mathbb P(
            H_N^{\mathrm c}
        )
        \le
        2N^{-\varepsilon}
    $,
    \bilingual{
        and thus
    }{
        从而
    }
    \begin{equation*}
        \mathbb P(
            H_N^{\mathrm c}
        )
        =
        O_N(N^{-\varepsilon}),
        \quad
        \lim_{N\to\infty}\mathbb P(H_N)=1.
    \end{equation*}
    \bilingual{
        By Cauchy--Schwarz inequality and the fact that $\ell_N=O_N(\log^3 N)$, we have
    }{
        根据 Cauchy--Schwarz 不等式和 $\ell_N=O_N(\log^3 N)$ 的事实,~ 有
    }
    \begin{align*}
        \mathbb E\left[
            \mathcal X_N^2
            {\bf 1}_{H_N^{\mathrm c}}
        \right]
        &
        \le
        \frac{
            \mathbb E\left[
                \left(
                    \mathcal M^N_{\ell_N}
                \right)^4
            \right]^{\frac 12}
            \!\!\!
            \cdot
            \mathbb P\!\left(
                H_N^{\mathrm c}
            \right)^{\frac 12}
        }{\log^2 N}
        =
        \frac{
            O_N(\ell_N^2)
            \cdot
            O_N(N^{-\varepsilon/2})
        }{\log^2 N}
        =
        o_N(1),
    \end{align*}
    \bilingual{
        and thus
    }{
        从而
    }
    \begin{equation*}
        \left|
            \mathbb E\left[
                \mathcal X_N
                {\bf 1}_{H_N^{\mathrm c}}
            \right]
        \right|
        \le
        \mathbb E\left[
            |\mathcal X_N|
            {\bf 1}_{H_N^{\mathrm c}}
        \right]
        \le
        \mathbb E\left[
            \mathcal X_N^2
            {\bf 1}_{H_N^{\mathrm c}}
        \right]^{\frac 12}
        =
        o_N(1).
    \end{equation*}
    \bilingual{
        On the other hand, by definition of $H_N$,
    }{
        另一方面,~ 根据 $H_N$ 的定义,
    }
    \begin{align*}
        \zeta\mathbb P(H_N)
        \le
        \mathbb E\left[
            \mathcal X_N
            {\bf 1}_{H_N}
        \right]
        \le
        \zeta'\mathbb P(H_N),
        \\
        \zeta^2\mathbb P(H_N)
        \le
        \mathbb E\left[
            \mathcal X_N^2
            {\bf 1}_{H_N}
        \right]
        \le
        \zeta'^2\mathbb P(H_N),
    \end{align*}
    \bilingual{
        Let $N\to\infty$, then $\zeta,\zeta'\to \mu$, we get
    }{
        令 $N\to\infty$,~ 其次令 $\zeta,\zeta'\to \mu$,~ 得到
    }
    \begin{align*}
        \lim_{N\to\infty}
        \mathbb E[ \mathcal X_N ]
        =
        \lim_{N\to\infty}
        \mathbb E[ \mathcal X_N {\bf 1}_{H_N} ]
        &
        =
        \mu,\\
        \lim_{N\to\infty}
        \mathbb E[ \mathcal X_N^2 ]
        =
        \lim_{N\to\infty}
        \mathbb E[ \mathcal X_N^2 {\bf 1}_{H_N} ]
        &
        =
        \mu^2,
    \end{align*}
    \bilingual{
        yielding \eqref{eq:4.13} and \eqref{eq:4.14}.
    }{
        从而得到 \eqref{eq:4.13} 和 \eqref{eq:4.14}.
    }

    \bilingual{
        We will first show \eqref{eq:4.15} for the case of $a\in(0,1]$ in the first sub-section, then for the case of $a=0$ in the second sub-section under the additional \Cref{assumption:moment-exp-1}, and finally show \eqref{eq:4.16} for all $a\in[0,1]$ in the third sub-section.
    }{
        下面将首先在第一小节中证明 \eqref{eq:4.15} 在 $a\in(0,1]$ 的情形下成立,~ 然后在第二小节中在额外的\Cref{assumption:moment-exp-1} 下证明 \eqref{eq:4.15} 对于 $a=0$ 的情形成立,~ 最后在第三小节中证明 \eqref{eq:4.16} 对于所有 $a\in[0,1]$ 的情形成立.
    }
\end{proof}

\subsection{\bilingual{Proof of the Polynomial Decay of the Right Tail for \texorpdfstring{$a\in(0,1]$}{a∈(0,1]}}{在 \texorpdfstring{$a\in(0,1]$}{a∈(0,1]} 的情形下右尾部多项式衰减的证明}}

\begin{proof}[\bilingual{Proof of the Polynomial Decay of the Right Tail for \texorpdfstring{$a\in(0,1]$}{a∈(0,1]}}{在 \texorpdfstring{$a\in(0,1]$}{a∈(0,1]} 的情形下右尾部多项式衰减的证明}]
    \quad

    \bilingual{
        We first show the polynomial decay of the right-hand side for the case of $a\in(0,1)$. In light of \Cref{lemma:4.8}, we construct a killed branching random walk $\mathcal V^{\mathrm{k}}$ (killed according to a given rule specified later). Denote $\mathcal M^{\mathrm{k}}_n:=\min_{|u|=n}\mathcal V^{\mathrm{k}}(u)$ for $n\ge 0$. So long as $\mathcal V^{\mathrm{k}}$ satisfies the conditions listed in \Cref{lemma:4.8}, we have
    }{
        先证明 \eqref{eq:4.15} 在 $a\in(0,1)$ 的情形下成立. ~根据\Cref{lemma:4.8},~ 构造一个经灭杀的分支随机游走 $\mathcal V^{\mathrm{k}}$ (灭杀规则见后文具体说明). ~对 $n\ge 0$ 记 $\mathcal M^{\mathrm{k}}_n:=\min_{|u|=n}\mathcal V^{\mathrm{k}}(u)$.~ 只要 $\mathcal V^{\mathrm{k}}$ 满足\Cref{lemma:4.8} 中列出的条件,~ 就有
    }
    \begin{equation*}
        \mathcal M^N_{\ell_N}
        \le
        \mathcal M^{\mathrm{k}}_{\ell_N}
        \quad
        \text{
            \bilingual{
                on the event $\{\tau>\ell_N\}$,
            }{
                在事件 $\{\tau>\ell_N\}$ 上,
            }
        }
    \end{equation*}
    \bilingual{
        where $\tau$ is the first time that the generation size of $\mathcal V^{\mathrm{k}}$ exceeds $N$. Note that the left-hand side in \eqref{eq:4.15} 
    }{
        其中 $\tau$ 是 $\mathcal V^{\mathrm{k}}$ 的粒子总数首次超过 $N$ 的时刻. 注意到 \eqref{eq:4.15} 的左侧
    }
    \begin{align}
        &\quad
        \mathbb P\left(
            \mathcal M^N_{\ell_N}
            \ge
            \zeta'\log N
        \right)
        \nonumber
        \\
        &
        =
        \mathbb P\left(
            \mathcal M^N_{\ell_N}
            \ge
            \zeta'\log N,\,
            \tau\le\ell_N
        \right)
        +
        \mathbb P\left(
            \mathcal M^N_{\ell_N}
            \ge
            \zeta'\log N,\,
            \tau>\ell_N
        \right)
        \nonumber
        \\ &
        \le
        \mathbb P\left(
            \tau\le\ell_N
        \right)
        +
        \mathbb P\left(
            \mathcal M^{\mathrm{k}}_{\ell_N}
            \ge
            \zeta'\log N
        \right).
        \label{eq:prop:3.1:1}
    \end{align}
    \bilingual{
        Therefore, we need to construct a $\mathcal V^{\mathrm{k}}$ such that, with high probability,
    }{
        因此需要构造一个 $\mathcal V^{\mathrm{k}}$,~ 使得以高概率成立
    }
    \begin{itemize}
        \item 
        \bilingual{
            $\tau>\ell_N$, i.e., the generation size of $\mathcal V^{\mathrm{k}}$ does not exceed $N$ till time $\ell_N$;
        }{
            $\tau>\ell_N$,~ 即 $\mathcal V^{\mathrm{k}}$ 的粒子总数在时刻 $\ell_N$ 之前不超过 $N$;
        }
        \item 
        \bilingual{
            $\mathcal M^{\mathrm{k}}_{\ell_N}<\zeta'\log N$, i.e., the minimum position of $\mathcal V^{\mathrm{k}}$ is below $\zeta'\log N$ at time $\ell_N$;
        }{
            $\mathcal M^{\mathrm{k}}_{\ell_N}<\zeta'\log N$,~ 即 $\mathcal V^{\mathrm{k}}$ 的最左侧位置在时刻 $\ell_N$ 时低于 $\zeta'\log N$;
        }
    \end{itemize}
    \bilingual{
        and, the probability of deviating from these two normal situations, i.e., the right-hand side of \eqref{eq:prop:3.1:1}, decays at least polynomially with respect to $N$. Note that, the left-hand side of \eqref{eq:prop:3.1:1} is naturally hard to compute, since the selection mechanism underlying the $N$-BRW requires that we keep track of the whole population at each step, meaning that when we are determining whether a particular particle should be preserved, we have to look at the positions of all other particles, including the descendants of different ancestors at time $0$, which is hard to analyze. On the other hand, the right-hand side of \eqref{eq:prop:3.1:1} is easier to compute, since we can construct $\mathcal V^{\mathrm{k}}$ with a simpler selection mechanism, so that when we are determining whether a particular particle should be preserved, we only need to look at the position of itself, and thus we can analyze $\mathcal V^{\mathrm{k}}$ by using \nameref{lemma:many-to-one} and Mogulskii's estimate for random walks.
    }{
        并且,~ 偏离上述两种正常情况的概率,~ 即 \eqref{eq:prop:3.1:1} 的右侧,~ 至少以 $N$ 的多项式速率衰减. 注意到,~ \eqref{eq:prop:3.1:1} 的左侧自然难以计算,~ 因为 $N$-分支随机游走底层的选择机制要求我们在每一步都要跟踪整个种群,~ 意味着当我们在确定一个特定粒子是否应该被保留时,~ 我们必须查看所有其他粒子的位置,~ 包括时刻 $0$ 不同祖先的后代的位置,~ 这很难分析. 另一方面,~ \eqref{eq:prop:3.1:1} 的右侧更容易计算,~ 因为我们可以构造一个具有更简单选择机制的 $\mathcal V^{\mathrm{k}}$,~ 使得当我们在确定一个特定粒子是否应该被保留时,~ 我们只需要查看它自己的位置,~ 从而我们可以使用\nameref{lemma:many-to-one}和 \nameref{theorem:mogulskii} 对随机游走小偏差的估计来分析 $\mathcal V^{\mathrm{k}}$.
    }

    \bilingual{
        We first state our construction of the killed branching random walk $\mathcal V^{\mathrm{k}}$. Fix $c\in(0,t^{-1/3})$, $b\in(0,a)$, and $v>0$ such that
    }{
        首先给出经灭杀的分支随机游走 $\mathcal V^{\mathrm{k}}$ 的构造. ~固定 $c\in(0,t^{-1/3})$, $b\in(0,a)$, 和 $v>0$ 使得
    }
    \begin{equation*}
        ct^{\frac 13}+(a-b)+v<1,\quad
        -b+\frac{\pi^2}2\sigma^2\left(c^{-2}t^{\frac 13}\right)+2v\left(1+t^{\frac 13}\right)<\zeta'.
    \end{equation*}
    \bilingual{
        Note that these inequalites are possible as $c\uparrow t^{-1/3}$, $b\uparrow a$, and $v\downarrow 0$. Also note that they are dependent on $a$, $t$, and $\zeta'$.
    }{
        注意到,~ 随着 $c\uparrow t^{-1/3}$,~ $b\uparrow a$,~ 和 $v\downarrow 0$,~ 这些不等式是可能满足的. 还要注意到它们依赖于 $a$,~ $t$,~ 和 $\zeta'$.
    }

    }{

    }{

    }

        \right)
        % \\ &

    \bilingual{
        which holds for $\varepsilon:=\frac 12(\varepsilon_1\wedge\varepsilon_2)>0$ and $N$ big enough. Therefore,

    \bilingual{

        % \\ &
        =

        \right)

            \frac{\pi^2}2\sigma^2
        \right)
        \frac{1}{\log^2 N}
        +
        o_N\left(\frac{1}{\log^2 N}\right),
    \end{align*}

        and let $t\to\infty$ to get the desired lower bound. We are now done with the proof of \Cref{theorem:speed}.
