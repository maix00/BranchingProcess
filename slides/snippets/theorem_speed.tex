\begin{theorem}[\texttt{theorem:speed}]
Consider a branching random walk $\mathcal V^{N|a}$ that starts with $\lfloor N^a\rfloor$ particles at position $0$, and whose branching law $\Xi$ satisfies the standing assumptions in the thesis (supercriticality, boundary case, finite variance, finite cross second moment, and moment conditions on $\Xi_1$).
Then the speed of the left-most particle exists and
\[
    v_N
    =
    \lim_{n\to\infty}\frac{1}{n}\mathcal M^{N|a}_n
    =
    \frac{\pi^2}{2}\sigma^2\frac{1}{\log^2 N}
    +
    o_N\!\left(\frac{1}{\log^2 N}\right).
\]
\end{theorem}
