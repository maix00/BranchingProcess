\begin{lemma}[Lemma 3.17 (Coupling of $N$-BRW and killed BRW)]
Let $\mathcal V^{\mathrm k}$ be the branching random walk $\mathcal V$ killed according to a given rule, such that the generation size process $\#\mathcal Z^{\mathrm k}$ is adapted to the natural filtration generated by $\mathcal V$.
Assume that at time $0$, the population of $\mathcal V^{\mathrm k}$ is included in (or equal to) that of $\mathcal V^N$.
Then there exists a coupling of $\mathcal V^N$ and $\mathcal V^{\mathrm k}$ such that
\[
    \mathcal M^N_k
    \le
    \mathcal M^{\mathrm k}_k,
    \qquad
    k\le\tau:=\inf\left\{
        k\ge 0:\ \#\mathcal Z^{\mathrm k}_k>N
    \right\}.
\]
\end{lemma}
