\textbf{Spine random walk.}
Under a change of measure (Lyons--Pemantle--Peres--Biggins), the BRW admits an \emph{infinite spine} --- a distinguished ray $Y_1,Y_2,\ldots$ --- whose increments are i.i.d.~with step distribution $\xi$ and log-mgf $\psi(\lambda)=\log\E[e^{\lambda\xi}]$. All particles branching off the spine form independent unconditioned BRWs.

\medskip
\textbf{Many-to-One Lemma (Lemma~2.1).}
For any $n\in\mathbb Z_+$ and measurable $g:\mathbb R^n\to\mathbb R_+$,
\[
    \mathbb E\Bigl[\sum_{|u|=n} g(\mathcal V(u_1),\ldots,\mathcal V(u_n))\Bigr]
    = \mathbb E\Bigl[e^{\lambda Y_n+\psi(\lambda)n}\,g(Y_1,\ldots,Y_n)\Bigr],
\]
where $\lambda>0$ with $\psi(\lambda)<\infty$. This transforms \textbf{sums over the tree} into \textbf{single-path probabilities} for $Y$, so large/small deviation theory (Mogul'ski\u{i}, Lemma~3.3) applies.
