% Lemma 3.17 role in the proof of Theorem 1.1.
\begin{block}{Lemma 3.17 (coupling $N$-BRW with a killed BRW)}
Constructs a coupling between $\mathcal V^N$ and a killed BRW $\mathcal V^{\mathrm k}$ (adapted killing rule) so that, up to the stopping time when $\#\mathcal Z^{\mathrm k}$ exceeds $N$, the minima satisfy a deterministic inequality.
This is the bridge turning BRW tube estimates into probability bounds for $\mathcal M^N_{\ell_N}$ (the two tail bounds in Theorem 1.1).
\end{block}
