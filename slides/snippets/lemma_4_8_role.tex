\begin{block}{Lemma \texttt{lemma:4.8} (coupling $N$-BRW with a killed BRW)}
Constructs a coupling between $\mathcal V^N$ and a killed BRW $\mathcal V^{\mathrm k}$ (adapted killing rule) so that, up to the stopping time when $\#\mathcal Z^{\mathrm k}$ exceeds $N$, the minima satisfy a deterministic inequality.
This is the bridge turning BRW tube estimates into probability bounds for $\mathcal M^N_{\ell_N}$ (the tail bounds \texttt{eq:4.15}/\texttt{eq:4.16}).
\end{block}
