\section{\bilingual{Background and Motivation}{研究背景}}

    \bilingual{
        Branching Random Walk (BRW) is a fundamental model in the theory of stochastic processes that describes the dynamic evolution of populations in space. From a mathematical construction perspective, BRW is not a simple collection of random walks (RW), but rather a complex random system composed of a family of random variables indexed on the Ulam--Harris--Neveu tree vertex space $\mathbb V$.
    }{
        {\kaishu 分支随机游走} (branching random walk, BRW) 是随机过程理论中刻画种群在空间中动态演化的核心模型. ~从数学构造上看,~ BRW 并非{\kaishu 随机游走} (random walk, RW) 的简单集合,~ 而是由一族索引在 {\kaishu Ulam--Harris--Neveu \mycite{Neveu1986} 树节点空间} $\mathbb V$ 上的随机变量构成的复杂随机系统.
    }

    \bilingual{
        Unlike random walks with independent and identically distributed increments, the defining feature of BRW is its significant {\itshape family correlation} or {\itshape hierarchical correlation} among particle displacements. Specifically, for two particles $u,v\in\mathbb V$ at generation $n$, the correlation strength between their spatial positions $V(u)$ and $V(v)$ depends strictly on their geometric distance in the hierarchical tree. If we denote $u\wedge v$ as their {\itshape most recent common ancestor} (MRCA), then the covariance between the two particles is proportional to the length of their shared ancestral path $|u\wedge v|$. This means that particles with closer ``kinship'' have more overlapping trajectories in space, and this nested hierarchical structure endows BRW with deep statistical dependencies.
    }{
        与具有独立同分布位移序列的随机游走不同,~ BRW 的核心特征在于其粒子位移具有显著的{\kaishu 家族相关性} (family correlation) 或{\kaishu 层次相关性} (hierarchical correlation). ~具体而言,~ 对于第 $n$ 代的两个粒子 $u,v\in\mathbb V$,~ 其空间位置 $V(u)$ 与 $V(v)$ 的相关性强度严格取决于它们在层级树中的几何距离. ~若记 $u\wedge v$ 为它们的{\kaishu 最近共祖} (most recent common ancestor, MRCA),~ 则两个粒子的协方差与它们共享的祖先路径长度 $|u\wedge v|$ 成正比. ~这意味着``血缘关系''越近的粒子,~ 其在空间中的运动轨迹重合度越高,~ 这种嵌套的层级结构使得 BRW 表现出极深的统计依赖性.
    }

    \bilingual{
        Due to this hierarchical correlation, BRW has been regarded as a discrete benchmark model for studying {\itshape log-correlated fields}. A log-correlated field is a class of random fields where the covariance between two points grows logarithmically with the inverse of their spatial distance. In a BRW with branching number $d$, the total number of particles at generation $n$ is $N = d^n$, which implies that the depth (hierarchy) of the tree satisfies $n = \log_d N$. Since the maximum variance (i.e., self-correlation) of the displacement between two particles is proportional to the generation $n$, the field intensity (variance) exhibits a logarithmic growth characteristic with respect to the system size $N$ (${\mathrm Var} \sim \log N$). This structure places BRW in the same universality class \mycite{Fyodorov_2012,arguin2016extremalogcorrelatedrandomvariables} as {\itshape two-dimensional Gaussian free fields} \mycite{bramson2015convergencelawmaximumtwodimensional}, {\itshape fluctuations of eigenvalues in random matrices}, and {\itshape behavior of the Riemann $\zeta$ function on the critical line} \mycite{najnudel2018extremevaluesriemannzeta,arguin2022maximarandommodelriemann}.
    }{
        正是由于这种层次相关性,~ BRW 已被视为研究{\kaishu 对数相关场} (log-correlated fields) 的离散基准模型. ~所谓对数相关场,~ 是指一类随机场,~ 其中两点之间的协方差随其空间距离的倒数呈对数级增长. ~在分支数为 $d$ 的 BRW 中,~ 第 $n$ 代的粒子总数 $N = d^n$,~ 这意味着树的深度 (层次) 满足 $n = \log_d N$. ~由于两个粒子位移的最大方差 (即自相关性) 正比于代数 $n$,~ 因此在种群规模为 $N$ 的系统中,~ 场强度 (方差) 相对于系统规模 $N$ 呈现出对数级的增长特征 (${\mathrm Var} \sim \log N$). ~这种结构使得 BRW 与{\kaishu 二维高斯自由场} \mycite{bramson2015convergencelawmaximumtwodimensional}、{\kaishu 随机矩阵特征值涨落}以及{\kaishu 数论中的 Riemann $\zeta$函数在临界线上的行为} \mycite{najnudel2018extremevaluesriemannzeta,arguin2022maximarandommodelriemann} 属于同一个{\kaishu 普适性类} (universality class) \mycite{Fyodorov_2012,arguin2016extremalogcorrelatedrandomvariables}.
    }

    \bilingual{
        Studying the extremal behavior of BRW (such as the position of the extremal particle) is essentially an exploration of how to find a precise probabilistic balance between the ``explosion'' caused by branching growth (exponential sample size) and the ``effective sparsity'' caused by hierarchical correlation. In contrast, in an independent and identically distributed sequence, the extremal behavior is entirely determined by the sample size; however, in BRW, due to the presence of hierarchical correlation, extremal particles often exhibit a {\itshape clustering} characteristic - the emergence of a ``genius'' particle is often accompanied by a collective emergence of its close relatives near the spatial frontier boundary. This correlation significantly alters the order of front advancement, leading to a slower sub-logarithmic delay compared to the independent case \mycite{Bramson1978MaximalDO,hu2009minimalpositioncriticalmartingale,Addario_Berry_2009,A_d_kon_2013,zeitouni2025log}. Therefore, BRW is not only a spatial model but also a window into understanding the universality of extremes in complex correlated systems.
    }{
        研究 BRW 的极值行为 (如极值位置),~ 本质上是在探索如何在分支增长导致的``数量爆炸” (指数级的样本量) 与层次相关性导致的``有效样本稀疏''之间寻找精确的概率平衡. ~作为比较,~ 在独立同分布序列中,~ 极值完全由样本量决定;~ 而在 BRW 中, 由于层次相关性的存在,~ 极值粒子往往呈现出{\kaishu 成簇} (clustering) 的特性——一个``天才''粒子的出现往往伴随着其近亲族群在空间前沿边界附近的集体涌现.~ 这种相关性极大地改变了前沿推进的阶数,~ 导致了比独立情形更慢的次阶对数延迟 \mycite{Bramson1978MaximalDO,hu2009minimalpositioncriticalmartingale,Addario_Berry_2009,A_d_kon_2013,zeitouni2025log}.~ 因此,~ BRW 不仅是一个空间模型,~ 更是理解复杂相关系统中极值普适性的窗口.
    }

    \bilingual{
        As a vibrant branch of stochastic process theory, BRW is not only an ideal mathematical model for studying sequences of random variables with hierarchical correlation, but also a core tool in statistical physics, evolutionary biology, and population genetics for characterizing spatiotemporal evolutionary dynamics. In non-equilibrium statistical physics, BRW has a profound duality with the nonlinear partial differential equation - the Fisher--KPP (Kolmogorov--Petrovsky--Piskunov) equation. The classical work of McKean~\mycite{mckean1975application} proved that the distribution function of the continuous version of BBM (branching Brownian motion) corresponds exactly to the solution of the KPP equation, thus linking the front propagation speed of the population to the intrinsic speed $v_\infty$ of the traveling wave solution. However, the deterministic KPP equation describes diffusion in a continuous medium, neglecting the stochastic fluctuations arising from the discrete {\itshape atomicity} of real physical systems. To simulate this microscopic effect, the $N$-branching random walk ($N$-BRW) model was introduced. Brunet and Derrida~\mycite{PhysRevE.56.2597} heuristically pointed out that under the constraint of finite population size $N$, there is a tiny ``cutoff effect'' on the front position; due to the exponential amplification effect of branching processes on front perturbations, this microscopic-level change ultimately leads to a significant $(\log N)^{-2}$ order correction in macroscopic wave speed.
    }{
        作为随机过程理论中极具生命力的分支,~ BRW 不仅是数学上研究具有层次相关性随机变量序列的理想模型,~ 更是统计物理、演化生物学及群体遗传学中刻画时空演化动力学的核心工具. ~在非平衡态统计物理中,~ BRW 与非线性偏微分方程——Fisher--KPP (Kolmogorov--Petrovsky--Piskunov) 方程有着深刻的对偶性. ~McKean~\mycite{mckean1975application} 的经典工作证明了BBW的连续版本{\kaishu 分支Brownian运动} (branching Brownian motion, BBM) 的分布函数恰好对应 KPP 方程的解,~ 从而将种群的前沿传播速度与行波解的本征速度 $v_\infty$ 联系起来.~ 然而,~ 确定性的 KPP 方程描述的是连续介质的扩散,~ 忽略了现实物理系统由于离散的{\kaishu 原子性} (atomicity) 而产生的随机涨落. ~为了模拟这种微观效应, {\kaishu $N$-分支随即游走} ($N$-branching random walk, $N$-BRW) 模型应运而生. ~Brunet 与 Derrida~\mycite{PhysRevE.56.2597} 启发式地指出,~ 在有限种群规模 $N$ 的限制下,~ 前沿位置会产生一个微小的``截断效应'';~ 由于分支过程对前沿扰动具有指数级的放大作用,~ 这一微观层面的改变最终导致宏观波速产生显著的 $(\log N)^{-2}$ 量级的修正. 
    }

    \bilingual{
        The significance of Brunet--Derrida's discovery lies in its challenge to the conventional wisdom in statistical physics that ``noise can be neglected in the large $N$ limit''. It revealed a non-trivial {\itshape scaling law}: even with an infinitesimal microscopic cutoff, due to the logarithmic sensitivity of front dynamics to discreteness, a significant macroscopic velocity shift can still occur. This finding forced the physics community to re-examine the limitations of deterministic differential equations in describing discrete living systems and physical fronts, and established the new universal paradigm of {\itshape noise-induced front shift}.
    }{
        Brunet--Derrida 的发现之所以具有里程碑意义,~ 是因为它打破了传统统计物理中``大$N$极限下噪声可忽略''的固有思维. 它揭示了一种非平凡的{\kaishu 标度律} (scaling law):~ 即使微观截断极小,~ 由于前沿动力学对离散性的对数级敏感性,~ 宏观速度仍会产生显著的偏移. ~这一发现迫使物理学界重新审视确定性微分方程在描述离散生命系统与物理前沿时的局限性,~ 并确立了{\kaishu 噪声诱导前沿偏移} (noise-induced front shift) 这一全新的普适性范式.
    }

    \bilingual{
        This discovery not only reshaped the understanding of nonlinear wave dynamics in statistical physics, but also exhibited profound universality significance in evolutionary biology and the physics of disordered systems. In evolutionary biology, $N$-BRW has become a standard model for simulating natural selection under limited resources. In this context, the spatial position $\mathcal V(u)$ of a particle is endowed with the biological meaning of {\itshape individual fitness}~\mycite{tsimring1996rna}. Although populations tend to expand in supercritical environments, the finite {\itshape environmental carrying capacity} $N$ enforces a selection pressure of ``survival of the fittest'': in each generation of reproduction, only the $N$ individuals with the highest fitness can survive and reproduce. This truncation mechanism not only fundamentally determines the macroscopic rate of adaptive evolution but also profoundly reshapes the {\itshape genealogical structure} of populations~\mycite{Brunet_2007,Berestycki_2013}. Through in-depth study of $N$-BRW, mathematical biologists have been able to quantitatively explain how populations maintain genetic diversity through front diffusion under natural selection pressure and reveal the dynamic characteristics of advantageous mutations in population expansion~\mycite{desai2007beneficial,PMID:23269838}. This interdisciplinary relevance indicates that $N$-BRW and its related models have become an important bridge connecting microscopic randomness, nonlinear dynamics, and macroscopic properties of complex systems.
    }{
        这一发现不仅重塑了统计物理关于非线性波动的认知,~ 更在演化生物学与无序系统物理学中展现出深远的普适性意义. ~在演化生物学领域,~ $N$-BRW 已成为模拟受限资源下自然选择的标准模型.~ 在该语境下,~ 粒子的空间位置 $\mathcal V(u)$ 被赋予了{\kaishu 个体适应度} (fitness)~\mycite{tsimring1996rna} 的生物学含义. ~虽然在超临界环境下种群具有扩张趋势,~ 但有限的{\kaishu 环境承载力} (carrying capacity) $N$ 强制执行了``优胜劣汰''的选择压力:~ 在每一代繁殖中,~ 仅有适应度最高的 $N$ 个个体才能得以生存并繁衍. ~这一截断机制不仅从根本上决定了适应性进化的宏观速率,~ 更深刻地重塑了种群的{\kaishu 系谱结构} (genealogical structure)~\mycite{Brunet_2007,Berestycki_2013}. ~通过对 $N$-BRW 的深入研究, ~生物数学家得以定量解释在自然选择压力下, ~种群如何通过前沿扩散维持遗传多样性,~ 并揭示优势突变在种群扩张中的动力学特征~\mycite{desai2007beneficial,PMID:23269838}. ~这种跨学科的关联性表明, ~$N$-BRW 及其相关模型已成为连接微观随机性、非线性动力学与复杂系统宏观性质的重要桥梁.
    }

\section{\bilingual{Related Work}{研究现状}}

    \bilingual{
        The study of extremal behavior in BRW has evolved from first-order asymptotic speed analysis to fine fluctuation analysis. Since the 1970s, Hammersley~\mycite{hammersley1974postulates}, Kingman~\mycite{kingman1975first}, and Biggins~\mycite{biggins1976first} established the first-order asymptotic speed of the extremal position in BRW. Subsequently, Bramson~\mycite{Bramson1978MaximalDO} discovered in his classic work on BBM that the front of the maximum position does not advance uniformly but has a famous second-order logarithmic correction term $-\frac 32\log n$, indicating that the extremal behavior of BRW has a more complex asymptotic structure than independent random walks.
    }{
        BRW 极值理论的研究经历了从一阶渐近速度到精细涨落分析的发展过程. ~自 20 世纪 70 年代起,~ Hammersley~\mycite{hammersley1974postulates},~ Kingman~\mycite{kingman1975first} 与 Biggins~\mycite{biggins1976first} 确立了 BRW 最值位置的一阶渐近速度. ~随后,~ Bramson~\mycite{Bramson1978MaximalDO} 在 BBM 的经典工作中发现,~ 最大位置前沿并非均匀推进,~ 而是存在一个著名的次阶对数修正项 $-\frac 32\log n$,~ 表明 BRW 的极值行为具有比独立随机游走更复杂的渐近结构.
    }

    \bilingual{
        The shift in research paradigm in this field largely relies on the innovation of probabilistic analysis tools, especially the maturation of the Lyons--Pemantle--Peres--Biggins spine decomposition theory~\mycite{lyons1995conceptual, biggins2004measure}. By constructing an infinite spine under change of measure, researchers have been able to transform the complex summation problem in tree space into the analysis of a single-path random walk (\nameref{lemma:many-to-one}), allowing for the use of large deviation and small deviation theories of random walks to precisely characterize the extremal properties of tree structures. This provides a core mathematical framework for quantitative analysis from macroscopic speed to microscopic structure.
    }{
        这一领域研究范式的转向,~ 很大程度上依赖于概率分析工具的革新,~ 特别是 Lyons--Pemantle--Peres--Biggins 脊柱分解 (spine decomposition) 理论~\mycite{lyons1995conceptual, biggins2004measure} 的成熟. ~通过构造测度变换下的无限长脊柱,~ 研究者得以将复杂的树状空间求和问题转化为单路径随机游走的分析 (\nameref{lemma:many-to-one}),~ 这一方法允许利用随机游走的大偏差理论和小偏差理论来精确刻画树状结构的极值性质. ~这为从宏观速度转向微观结构的定量解析提供了核心数学框架.
    }

    \bilingual{
        Under this framework,~ the research focus after the 21st century has shifted to the weak convergence properties of extremal particles. A\"id\'ekon~\mycite{A_d_kon_2013} combined the derivative martingale technique with change of measure methods to prove that the extremal position has a non-degenerate weak limit after second-order correction. Subsequently, Madaule~\mycite{madaule2017convergence} and others further characterized the extremal process, proving its convergence to a decorated Poisson point process (DPPP), thus completing a comprehensive mathematical description of the microscopic structure of BRW extremes.
    }{
        这一框架下,~ 21 世纪后研究重点转向极值的弱收敛性质. ~A\"id\'ekon~\mycite{A_d_kon_2013} 结合了{\kaishu 导数鞅} (derivative martingale) 技术与测度变换的方法,~ 证明了极值位置在经过次阶修正后具有非退化的弱极限. ~此后,~ Madaule~\mycite{madaule2017convergence} 等人进一步刻画了极值点过程 (extremal process),~ 证明其收敛至一个{\kaishu 装饰Poisson点过程} (decorated Poisson point process, DPPP),~ 完成了对 BRW 极值微观结构的完整数学描述.
    }

    \bilingual{
        As for the $N$-BRW model with selection mechanism,~ the core issue is to verify the conjecture proposed by Brunet and Derrida~\mycite{Brunet_2007} regarding the velocity correction term:
    }{
        对于带选择机制的 $N$-BRW 模型,~ 核心课题是验证 Brunet 与 Derrida 等~\mycite{Brunet_2007} 提出的关于速度修正项的猜想:
    }
    \begin{equation*}
        \frac{C}{\left(\log N+3\log\log N+o_N(\log\log N)\right)^2},
    \end{equation*}
    \bilingual{
        where $C$ is an explicit constant determined by the branching law. The first challenge is to rigorously establish the $(\log N)^{-2}$ scaling law of the velocity correction term with respect to the population size $N$. Bérard and Gouéré~\mycite{bérard2010brunetderridabehaviorbranchingselectionparticle} were the first to mathematically prove the correctness of this asymptotic order. The applicability of this result is limited to binary branching models where each generation produces two identically distributed offspring. Subsequently, Mallein~\mycite{Mallein_2017} further extended the study to the convergence of $v_N$ under general $\alpha$-stable displacement distributions, establishing its universal $(\log N)^{-2}$ scaling law. Secondly, the formula implies the existence of a secondary correction term, namely $(\log N)^{-3}\log\log N$.~ Although this fine term has a clear physical meaning (reflecting the logarithmic delay characteristic of the front particle {\kaishu cloud}), mathematically, except for a few specific soft selection models that can be solved exactly~\mycite{cortines2016nbranchingrandomwalkrandom}, proving this higher-order expansion term under the original hard selection $N$-BRW framework remains an open problem.
    }{
        其中$C$是由分支律决定的显式常数. ~首先面临的问题是严格确立速度修正项相对于种群规模 $N$ 的 $(\log N)^{-2}$ 标度律. Bérard 与 Gouéré~\mycite{bérard2010brunetderridabehaviorbranchingselectionparticle} 首次在数学上证明了该渐近阶数的正确性. ~该结果的适用范围限定于每代产生两个同分布子代的二分支模型. ~此后,~ Mallein~\mycite{Mallein_2017} 进一步将研究拓展到一般$\alpha$-稳定位移分布下 $v_N$ 的收敛性,~ 确立了其普遍的$(\log N)^{-2}$ 标度律. ~其次, ~该公式暗示了次级修正项的存在,~ 即 $(\log N)^{-3}\log\log N$.~ 虽然这一精细项在物理学中具有明确的含义 (反映了前沿粒子{\kaishu 云团} (cloud) 的对数延迟特征), 但在数学上,~ 除了少数可精确求解的特定软选择模型~\mycite{cortines2016nbranchingrandomwalkrandom} 外,~ 在原始的硬选择 $N$-BRW 框架下证明该高阶展开项仍然是一个开放问题.
    }

    \bilingual{
        Another research direction for $N$-BRW is its genealogical structure traced back in time. In neutral evolutionary models without selection pressure, the {\itshape genealogy} of the population typically follows the {\itshape Kingman coalescent}; however, in $N$-BRW with strong selection pressure, many individuals in the current generation share an extremely outstanding {\itshape super-ancestor}, leading to multiple branches coalescing simultaneously when tracing back in time. This feature of {\itshape multiple collisions} corresponds to the {\itshape Bolthausen--Sznitman coalescent} (BS coalescent), which was first predicted by physicists~\mycite{Brunet_2007, Brunet_2012}. Mathematically, Berestycki et al.~\mycite{Berestycki_2013} rigorously proved this conclusion for $N$-BBM with absorbing boundaries. However, for $N$-BRW under hard selection mechanism, the asymptotic behavior of its genealogical structure and the rigorous proof of the coalescence time scale still face technical difficulties.
    }{
        $N$-BRW 的另一研究方向是其随时间回溯的系谱结构. ~在无选择压力的中性演化模型中,~ {\kaishu 系谱} (genealogy)通常遵循 {\kaishu Kingman 聚合} (Kingman coalescent);~ 但在具有强选择压力的$N$-BRW 中,~ 当前某一代的很多个体都共有一个极其出色的{\kaishu 超级祖先} (super-ancestor),~ 使得在回溯时间时,~ 多个分支会同时聚合,~ 这种具有{\kaishu 多体碰撞} (multiple collisions) 特征的 {\kaishu Bolthausen--Sznitman 聚合} (BS coalescent) 首先被物理学家预言~\mycite{Brunet_2007, Brunet_2012}. ~在数学层面,~ Berestycki 等~\mycite{Berestycki_2013} 为带吸收边界的$N$-分支Brownian运动严格证明了这一结论. ~然而,~ 对于硬选择机制下的 $N$-BRW,~ 其系谱结构的渐近行为以及合并时间尺度的严格证明仍面临技术困难.
    }

    \bilingual{
        Recently, the research focus on $N$-BRW has shifted from order estimation of macroscopic speed to fine characterization of frontier dynamic trajectories and edge fluctuations. A\"id\'ekon and Hu~\mycite{aïdékon2025averageweightpercolationcompletegraph} studied a class of $N$-branching random walks with a specific Poisson structure, establishing an $\mathrm L^2$ limit theorem for the position of the leftmost particle under asymmetric scaling of triple logarithmic time and single logarithmic space, proving that its position follows a linear trajectory during evolution, thus quantitatively revealing the influence of initial population size on the evolutionary path of the frontier, providing a new mathematical framework for understanding the stability of constrained branching systems.
    }{
        近期,~ 关于 $N$-BRW 的研究重心已从宏观速度的阶数估计转向对前沿动态轨迹及边缘涨落的精细刻画. ~A\"id\'ekon 与 Hu \mycite{aïdékon2025averageweightpercolationcompletegraph} 针对一类具有特定 Poisson 结构的 $N$-分支随机游走,~ 在三次对数时间与一次对数空间的非对称缩放尺度下,~ 确立了最左侧粒子位置的 $\mathrm L^2$极限定理,~ 证明其位置在演化过程中遵循线性轨迹,~ 从而定量揭示了初始种群规模对前沿演化路径的影响,~ 这为理解受限分支系统的稳定性提供了新的数学框架.
    }

\chapter{\bilingual{Conclusions and Future Work}{结论与展望}}

\section{\bilingual{Conclusions}{结论}}
\label{section:conclusion-and-prospects}

\bilingual{
    This thesis studies the asymptotic evolutionary behavior of $N$-branching random walks with general branching laws under the framework of universality class with finite variance ($\alpha=2$). By introducing a second moment constraint for the cross terms of the branching law (\Cref{assumption:log-expt-cross-sum-exp-neg-sum}) and a moment constraint for the leftmost particle (\Cref{assumption:moment-exp-1}), we obtained the following main conclusions:
}{
    本文在有限方差 ($\alpha=2$) 的普适性类框架下,~ 研究了具有一般分支律的 $N$-分支随机游走的渐近演化行为. ~通过引入针对分支律交叉项的二阶矩约束 (\Cref{assumption:log-expt-cross-sum-exp-neg-sum}) 及最左侧粒子的矩约束 (\Cref{assumption:moment-exp-1}) ,~ 本文得到了以下主要结论：
}

\begin{enumerate}
    \item 
    \bilingual{
        \textbf{Universality Extension of the Trajectory Limit Theorem}: 

        This paper successfully extends the $L^2$ limit theorem for the position of the leftmost particle originally established by A\"id\'ekon and Hu~\mycite{aïdékon2025averageweightpercolationcompletegraph} for a specific Poisson point process branching law to the case of general distributions satisfying moment conditions. The result shows that if the time axis is taken to be of the order $t\log^3 N$ and the one-dimensional space axis is taken to be of the order $\log N$, then in this asymmetrically logarithmic scaled time-space, there exists a line $-a+\frac{\pi^2}2\sigma^2t$ such that the position of the leftmost particle in the $N$-branching random walk converges in $L^2$ to the corresponding point on this line at each vertical time slice $t$ as $N$ increases. This result quantitatively reveals the influence of the initial number of particles $\lfloor N^a \rfloor$ on the evolution position of the frontier and confirms that this linear trajectory is an intrinsic property of finite variance $N$-BRW systems.
    }{
        \textbf{轨迹极限定理的普适性推广}:~ 
        本文成功将 A\"id\'ekon 与 Hu \mycite{aïdékon2025averageweightpercolationcompletegraph} 原本针对特定 Poisson 点过程分支律所建立的 $L^2$ 极限定理,~ 推广至满足一般矩条件的分布情形. ~该结论表明:~ 若将时间轴取为 $t\log^3 N$ 的数量级,~ 一维空间轴取为 $\log N$ 的数量级,~ 那么在这一经不对称对数缩放的时空中,~ 存在一条直线 $-a+\frac{\pi^2}2\sigma^2t$,~ 使得 $N$-分支随机游走的最左侧位置,~ 在每一个纵向的时间切片 $t$ 处,~ 随着 $N$ 增加而在 $L^2$ 意义下收敛到该直线上对应的点. ~这一结果定量地给出了初始粒子数 $\lfloor N^a \rfloor$ 对前沿演化位置的影响,~ 并证实了该线性轨迹是有限方差 $N$-BRW 系统的本征属性.
    }

    \item 
    \bilingual{
        \textbf{Application and Validation of the Mogul'ski\u i Small Deviation Estimation Framework}: 

        In terms of technical implementation, this paper follows the small deviation probability estimation framework established by A\"id\'ekon and Hu~\mycite{aïdékon2025averageweightpercolationcompletegraph}. Specifically, using \Cref{lemma:4.1,lemma:4.2} to establish upper bounds on the small deviation probabilities of random walks within horizontal and tilted tubes, and using \Cref{lemma:4.3} to establish corresponding lower bounds on the probabilities. The work in this paper proves that this estimation scheme based on exponential measure change and \nameref{theorem:mogulskii} is still effective under general branching laws, providing reliable technical support for handling second moment estimates in models with non-specific distribution laws.
    }{
        \textbf{\nameref{theorem:mogulskii} 小偏差估计框架的应用与验证}:
        在技术实现上,~ 本文沿用了 A\"id\'ekon 与 Hu \mycite{aïdékon2025averageweightpercolationcompletegraph} 建立的小偏差概率估计框架。其中,~ 利用\Cref{lemma:4.1,lemma:4.2} 确立随机游走在水平及倾斜管道内的小偏差概率上界,~ 并利用\Cref{lemma:4.3} 确立相应的概率下界. ~本文的工作证明了这一套基于指数测度变换与 \nameref{theorem:mogulskii} 的估计方案在一般分支律下依然有效,~ 为处理非特定分布律模型的二阶矩估计提供了可靠的技术支撑.
    }

    \item
    \bilingual{
        \textbf{Technical Improvement in the $a=0$ Case Proof}: 

        In proving the limit theorem starting from a single particle ($a=0$), this paper improves the proof strategy of A\"id\'ekon and Hu~\mycite{aïdékon2025averageweightpercolationcompletegraph}. To address the extinction risk that may arise after truncation for general branching laws, this paper utilizes the supercritical assumption to construct a at most binary branching process through displacement truncation $M$, and introduces a {\kaishu restart mechanism}, combined with martingale maximal inequalities to control position deviations. Under this construction, the time $T$ required for the population size to reach $O(N^\alpha)$ is of order $O(\log N)$, thus ensuring the stability of the limit trajectory at the main scale.
    }{
        \textbf{$a=0$ 情形证明的技术改进}:~ 
        在证明从单个粒子出发 ($a=0$) 的极限定理时,~ 本文改进了 A\"id\'ekon 与 Hu \mycite{aïdékon2025averageweightpercolationcompletegraph} 的证明策略. ~针对一般分支律在截断处理后可能产生的灭绝风险,~ 本文利用超临界假设,~ 通过位移截断 $M$ 构造了至多二分叉过程,~ 并引入了{\kaishu 重启机制},~ 并结合下鞅最大值不等式控制了位置偏移. ~在该构造下,~ 种群规模达到 $O(N^\alpha)$ 所需的时间 $T$ 为 $O(\log N)$ 数量级,~ 从而确保了极限轨迹在主尺度下的稳定性.
    }

    \item \bilingual{
        \textbf{Comparative Evaluation with Mallein's Conclusion}: 

        Compared with Mallein's study~\mycite{Mallein_2017} on the speed $v_N$, this paper obtains a stronger $L^2$ convergence conclusion at the cost of sacrificing some distribution universality (requiring exponential moments to control variance). Mallein established the almost sure limit of the speed $v_N$ under a wider second moment condition for the case $\alpha \in (0, 2]$; while this paper, for the case $\alpha=2$, through fine second moment analysis, verifies the $(\log N)^{-2}$ scaling law of the velocity correction term and gives an explicit constant, and fully characterizes the dynamic evolution path of the frontier during the establishment of equilibrium.
    }{
        \textbf{与 Mallein 结论的对比评价}:~ 
        与 Mallein \mycite{Mallein_2017} 关于速度 $v_N$ 的研究相比,~ 本文在牺牲一定分布普适性的前提下 (要求指数矩以控制方差) ,~ 获得了强度更高的 $L^2$ 收敛结论. ~Mallein 在较宽的二阶矩条件下确立了 $\alpha \in (0, 2]$ 情形下速度 $v_N$ 的几乎处处极限；而本文针对 $\alpha=2$ 情形,~ 通过精细的二阶矩分析,~ 验证了速度修正项的 $\log^{-2} N$ 标度律并给出了显式常数,~ 并完整刻画了前沿在平衡态建立过程中的动态演化路径.
    }
\end{enumerate}

\section{\bilingual{Future Work}{展望}}

    \bilingual{
        Although this paper establishes the $L^2$ limit trajectory of $N$-BRW under general branching laws, due to the limitations of current mathematical tools and the strength of assumptions, there are still the following directions worth further exploration:
    }{
        本文确立了一般分支律下 $N$-BRW 的 $L^2$ 极限轨迹,~ 但受限于目前的数学工具与假设强度,~ 仍有以下方向值得进一步探索:
    }

    \begin{enumerate}
        \item 
        \bilingual{
            \textbf{Rigorous Proof of Higher-Order Velocity Correction Terms}: 

            Physicists Brunet and Derrida~\mycite{Brunet_2007} proposed a more refined conjectured value for the velocity correction term:
        }{
            \textbf{高阶速度修正项的严格证明}:~ 
            物理学家 Brunet 与 Derrida 等~\mycite{Brunet_2007} 曾提出关于速度修正项更精细的猜想值:~ 
        }
        \begin{equation*}
            v_\infty - v_N = C{\left(\log N+3\log\log N+o_N(\log\log N)\right)^{-2}}.
        \end{equation*}
        \bilingual{
            This formula suggests that the second-order term should have order $O(\frac{\log \log N}{\log^3 N})$. ~Under the $L^2$ analysis framework adopted in this paper, exploring how to capture this term through more refined control of edge effects or higher-order moment estimates is a challenging open problem in this field.
        }{
            该公式暗示其二阶项的量级应为 $O(\frac{\log \log N}{\log^3 N})$. ~在本文采用的 $L^2$ 分析框架下,~ 探讨如何通过更精细的管道边缘效应控制或更高阶的矩估计来捕捉这一项,~ 是该领域具有挑战性的开放课题.
        }

        \item 
        \bilingual{
            \textbf{Trajectory Limit Theorem under Heavy-Tailed Branching Laws}: 

            For the case of stable distributions with $\alpha < 2$, Mallein~\mycite{Mallein_2017} has given the scaling law of the first-order speed. However, for this universality class, the $L^2$ trajectory characterization of the frontier position in logarithmically scaled time-space is still unknown. Due to the significantly enhanced fluctuations of the spine random walk under heavy-tailed distributions, traditional second moment analysis methods may face failure. Exploring probability estimation tools suitable for stable processes to establish fine trajectory limits under heavy-tailed scenarios has theoretical significance.
        }{
            \textbf{厚尾分支律下的轨迹极限定理}:~ 
            对于 $\alpha < 2$ 的稳定分布情形,~ Mallein \mycite{Mallein_2017} 已给出一阶速度的标度律. ~然而,~ 在这一普适性类下,~ 前沿位置在对数缩放时空中的 $L^2$ 轨迹特征尚未可知. ~由于厚尾分布下脊柱随机游走的波动性显著增强,~ 传统的二阶矩分析方法可能面临失效. ~探索适用于稳定过程的概率估计工具,~ 以确立厚尾情形下的精细轨迹极限,~ 具有理论意义.
        }

        \item 
        \bilingual{
            \textbf{Coupling Construction Based on Mogul'ski\u i Regions}: 

            \nameref{theorem:mogulskii} defines the small deviation probability of a random walk falling within a specific function region. A potential research direction is whether, for other types of models with selection pressure, one can establish the trajectory limit theorem of the frontier by constructing auxiliary random processes coupled within specific regions. This region-constrained coupling construction method is expected to extend the analytical framework of this paper to more branching systems with atypical selection mechanisms.
        }{
            \textbf{基于 Mogul'ski\u i 区域的耦合构造}:~ 
            \nameref{theorem:mogulskii} 定义了随机游走落在特定函数区域内的小偏差概率.~ 一个潜在的研究方向是:~ 能否针对其他类型的带有选择压力的模型,~ 通过在特定的区域内构造与之耦合的辅助随机过程,~ 来确立其前沿的轨迹极限定理. 这种基于区域约束的耦合构造方法,~ 有望将本文的分析框架推广至更多非典型选择机制的分支系统.
        }

        \item 
        \bilingual{
            \textbf{Genealogical Merging and Bolthausen--Sznitman Coalescence under Hard Selection}: 

            A core conjecture of $N$-BRW is that its genealogical structure converges to the Bolthausen--Sznitman coalescence as $N \to \infty$. Although this conjecture has been proven in models such as BBM with absorbing walls, for the original "hard selection" $N$-BRW, the proof remains an open problem due to the complex global ranking dependencies among particles. The frontier stability estimates established in this paper provide a potential foundation for analyzing the time scale of genealogical merging (expected to be $O(\log^3 N)$). By studying the evolution of super-ancestors, progress in this direction is anticipated.
        }{
            \textbf{硬选择机制下的系谱合并与 Bolthausen--Sznitman 聚合}:~ 
            $N$-BRW 的一个核心猜想是其系谱结构在 $N \to \infty$ 时收敛至 Bolthausen--Sznitman 聚合. ~虽然该猜想已在带吸收壁的 BBM 等模型中得到证明,~ 但对于原始的``硬选择'' $N$-BRW,~ 由于粒子间存在复杂的全局排名依赖,~ 其证明仍然是该领域的开放问题. ~本文所确立的前沿稳定性估计为分析系谱合并的时间标度 (预计为 $O(\log^3 N)$) 提供了潜在的基础支撑,~ 通过研究超级祖先的演化,~ 该方向有望有所进展.
        }
        
        \item 
        \bilingual{
            \textbf{Extension to Non-Homogeneous Settings and Multi-Dimensional Spaces}:

            In realistic models, the branching law often varies with spatial location or time (non-homogeneous environments). Moreover, in multi-dimensional spaces, $N$-BRW can exhibit complex "frontier shape" evolution. Extending the second moment framework of this paper to multi-dimensional spaces or branching models with temporal correlations will provide a more universal mathematical description for frontier dynamics in biological invasions and statistical physics.
        }{
            \textbf{非均匀环境与多维空间的扩展}:~ 
            现实模型中的分支律往往随空间位置或时间发生变化 (非齐次环境). ~此外,~ 多维空间中的 $N$-BRW 会表现出复杂的``前沿形状''演化. ~将本文的二阶矩框架推广至多维空间或具有时间相关性的分支模型,~ 将为生物入侵和统计物理中的前沿动力学提供更具普适性的数学描述.
        }
    \end{enumerate}
